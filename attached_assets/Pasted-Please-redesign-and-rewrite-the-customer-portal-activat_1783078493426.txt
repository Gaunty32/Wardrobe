Please redesign and rewrite the customer portal activation email.

The current email feels too dull and transactional. We want it to feel exciting, professional and like the launch of a valuable new service.

This email is for customers whose bespoke Select Branding Solutions ordering portal has been created.

Key requirements:

Include the Select Branding Solutions full-colour logo at the top, alongside or above the customer’s logo. This should feel like a partnership between Select Branding Solutions and the customer, not just a standard system email.

The email should explain that their personal uniform ordering portal is now live.

Tone should be:
Warm, professional, confident, excited and customer-focused.

Main message:
This is not just an ordering website. It is a bespoke uniform management portal designed around their business.

The email should highlight that the customer can:

Order branded workwear quickly and easily
Track orders in real time
View previous orders
Manage company wardrobe requirements
Save employee sizing and preferences
Order for named employees
Use the portal on desktop, tablet or mobile
Download/install it as a mobile app
Request new products or logo setups when needed
